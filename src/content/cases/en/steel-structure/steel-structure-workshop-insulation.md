## Project Background

Modern pre-engineered steel buildings require advanced thermal envelopes. This case study details the thermal and acoustic insulation installation for a massive manufacturing plant built completely with a steel frame structure, covering a total roof and wall surface area of over 45,000 square meters.

## The Challenge

- **The "Oven Effect":** Uninsulated steel structures absorb immense solar radiation in summer, turning the interior into a high-temperature zone, which hinders manufacturing labor.
- **Condensation on Steel Sheets:** Thermal bridging on metal panels during winter causes condensation, leading to interior rain effects, destroying machinery, and accelerating rust.
- **Industrial Noise Transmission:** Heavy rain impacts on large metal roofs create loud interior noise levels that disrupt industrial operations.

![Steel Structure Roof Glass Wool Insulation Installation](/images/cases/workshop-roof.jpg)

## Our Solution

We supplied high-performance **Glass Wool Blankets with Factory-Applied FSK Facing** designed specifically for roll-out application over steel purlins.

- **Superior Thermal Barrier:** The glass wool matrix acts as a powerful buffer against solar radiant heat transfer, keeping the workshop interior significantly cooler.
- **Integrated Vapor Barrier (FSK Facing):** The reinforced aluminum foil facing provides a seamless, tough moisture-vapor barrier, stopping indoor humid air from touching the cold outer metal sheets.
- **Excellent Sound Absorption:** The soft, fibrous network of the glass wool blankets absorbs the mechanical kinetic energy of rain impacts, deadening noise transfer.

## The Result

- **Substantial HVAC Cost Efficiency:** The workshop achieved an energy-saving rate of over 18% on seasonal indoor cooling and heating operations.
- **100% Elimination of Condensation:** The internal steel roof remained completely dry throughout winter, ensuring zero water damage to high-precision CNC machinery below.
- **Enhanced Acoustics:** Indoor noise levels during heavy rainfall dropped by over 20 dB, creating a compliant and productive working environment for factory workers.
