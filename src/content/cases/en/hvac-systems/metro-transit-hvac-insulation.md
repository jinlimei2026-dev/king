## Project Background

Rapid urban transit environments demand extraordinary reliability from their subterranean infrastructure. This project covers the comprehensive HVAC and ventilation system insulation for a newly constructed municipal metro line, encompassing 14 underground stations and associated tunnels.

## The Challenge

Underground metro HVAC systems operate under extreme conditions:

- **High-Velocity Airflow:** Ventilation ducts handle massive air volumes, causing severe vibration and acoustic resonance.
- **Confined Space and Humidity:** Subterranean air tunnels naturally harbor high humidity, increasing the risk of mold growth and metal duct corrosion.
- **Extreme Fire Safety Standards:** Underground transit systems enforce zero-tolerance fire regulations regarding toxic smoke and flame propagation.

![Underground Metro Station Ventilation Ducts](/images/cases/metro-ducts-insulation.jpg)

## Our Solution

Our engineering team implemented a combined system utilizing high-density **Elastomeric Rubber Foam Sheets** for the air handling systems.

- **High-Density Acoustic Dampening:** The dense elastomeric matrix effectively absorbs structural vibration and mechanical noise generated by large commercial air handling units (AHUs).
- **Anti-Microbial & Vapor Proof:** The completely non-porous surface of our closed-cell rubber material naturally resists moisture ingress and prevents mold or bacterial colonizations, maintaining clean airflow.
- **Advanced Fire Retardant Properties:** The customized rubber material conforms to strict underground safety norms, releasing minimal smoke toxicity and maintaining self-extinguishing capabilities under direct flame exposure.

## The Result

- **Optimized Air Distribution Efficiency:** Thermal loss across the subterranean duct network was minimized, ensuring precise climate control at station platforms.
- **Acoustic Comfort achieved:** Noise levels at the platform and passenger waiting areas dropped by an average of 12 dB, significantly improving commuter experience.
- **Longevity & Low Maintenance:** The anti-aging properties of our PVC/NBR blend insulation ensure it will not crack or degrade over decades of underground service, eliminating frequent maintenance costs.
